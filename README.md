# Eat & Split 🍕

I made this because splitting the bill after dinner with friends always turns into a maths fight. Everyone orders different things, someone gets an expensive meal, someone just has a drink, and the tax comes as one big number at the end.

This app fixes that. Just type what each person ate, and it tells you who owes how much.

## What it can do
- **Split evenly:** everyone pays the same, tip and tax included.
- **Pay for your own plate:** the person who ate more pays more tax and tip. The person who ate less pays less.
- **Find the tax by itself:** if the bill only shows the final total, it works out the tax for you.
- **Shared food:** fries or drinks that everyone shared get split equally.
- **Exact total:** the shares always add up to the bill, not even 1 paisa short.
- **Works on phone:** you can use it right at the table.

## How to use it
**Website:** download the files and open `index.html` in your browser. Keep `style.css` and `script.js` in the same folder, or it won't look right.

**Python version:** open the terminal and run:
```
python3 task3.py
```

## Example
Three friends order ₹200, ₹500 and ₹300. The bill total is ₹1050.
The app finds that ₹50 is tax and shares it fairly: they pay ₹210, ₹525 and ₹315.

## Made with
HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Python. No extra libraries needed.
